# Painting a pebble

> A small stone in 3D that you turn in your fingers and paint on every side:
> the top, the bottom and all around.

## What it is

The pebble editor is where a pebble gets its look before you publish it. You
pick a stone, paint it, and when you tap the tick the editor hands back three
things: the design, the look printed on the card (shape, seed, material and
two main colours) and a tiny 16×16 picture of the top.

## How it works

**The stone.** Every stone is made on your device from a shape and a number
(the *seed*). The shape is one of 28: a round river stone, a flat skipping
stone, an egg, a heart, a tall one, a wide one, one with a hollow, a bean, a
peanut, a dice and more. **Kamień** also has a button for another stone of
the same shape, sliders for width, height and length, 40 materials (granite,
river stones with quartz bands, marble, sea glass, gloss and matte paints,
metallic markers) and the stone's own colour. Each material takes the light
in its own way: varnish shines, sea glass glows at the edges, metal reflects
a bright horizon.

**Turning it.** Drag to turn the stone any way you like; it keeps spinning a
little when you let go. Pinch to zoom. **Góra**, **Dół**, **Przód** and
**Tył** turn it to face you; **Boki** goes round the sides one by one.
**Podgląd** spins it slowly like a shop window (not when your system asks for
less motion).

**Painting.** The stone's surface is split into six faces (top, bottom,
front, back, left and right), and you paint straight on it: where your
finger touches, the paint lands. A stroke that crosses from one face to the
next carries on across the seam. While painting, two fingers still turn and
zoom the stone.

- **Pędzel** and **Spray** in five sizes and 33 colours; **Gumka** brings the
  bare stone back.
- **Lustro** paints the mirror image on the other half at the same time.
- **Wypełnij** fills the area you tap, up to the brush strokes around it, or
  a whole face; the button under it paints the faces chosen above.
- **Naklejki**: over 140 stamps in eleven groups (animals, insects with
  butterflies and ladybirds, flowers, leaves, stars, hearts, faces, weather,
  food, shapes, letters and digits), in their own colours or yours, of any
  size and turn.
- **Napis** writes along the surface, straight or bent into an arc, in four
  typefaces.
- **Wzory** lays one of 20 patterns (dots, stripes, checks, mandala, waves,
  zigzags, scales, confetti and more) over the chosen faces.
- **Gotowe wzory**: 26 finished pebbles (ladybird, watermelon, planet, owl,
  cat, flower, rainbow, bee, galaxy, sea glass, mandala and others) to use as
  they are or as a start.
- **Losuj** makes a new random design. **Cofnij** and **Ponów** step back and
  forward through your changes.

## What stays private

The editor works entirely on your device. Nothing leaves it until you
publish the pebble.

## Limits

- The design is kept as painting steps (strokes, stamps, fills, text and
  patterns), not as a picture, and may take at most 64 KB. A typical pebble
  takes a few kilobytes; when a stone is full, the editor says so and you
  can step back.
- The card's top-view picture is only 16×16 dots in 16 colours, so a finder
  sees a rough preview until the full design arrives.
- Turning the stone by tilting the phone is not available.
