# Code of conduct

Zen is made for people who trust each other. The places where Zen itself
is made and talked about (this repository, its issues and pull requests,
and [Zen on Discord](https://discord.gg/g4vHvpKV37)) should feel the
same.

## What we ask of everyone

- Be kind and patient, also with people who are new, who write in another
  language, or who see things differently.
- Talk about the work, not about the person: say what is wrong with a
  change and how it could be better.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you judge.
- Respect privacy: never post someone's personal details, addresses,
  keys or messages without their clear consent.
- Help keep it calm: a heated thread is better paused than won.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination or hostility because of who someone is: age, body,
  disability, ethnicity, gender, identity, nationality, religion, sexual
  orientation, experience or anything else.
- Sexual language or images, and content meant to shock.
- Trolling, spam, and deliberately disrupting a discussion.
- Pushing anyone to use Zen to watch, follow or control another person.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and
messages that break these rules, and may warn, mute or ban the people who
wrote them, for a while or for good, in this repository and on Discord.
Decisions are made by looking at what happened, not at who it was.

## Reporting

Tell the maintainer privately: a direct message on
[Discord](https://discord.gg/g4vHvpKV37) (to a moderator or to the
maintainer). Say what happened, where and when, with links or
screenshots if you have them. Reports are kept confidential, and nobody
is punished for reporting in good faith.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
