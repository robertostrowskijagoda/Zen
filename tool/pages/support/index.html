What to attach

  • Your Zen version and system (Settings → About).
  • What you did, what happened and what you expected.
  • The technical log: Settings → Diagnostics shows the latest entries, and the save button writes the whole log to a file. It holds no message content, names or addresses.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Hide private information first.
