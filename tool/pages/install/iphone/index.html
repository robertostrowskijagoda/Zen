Zen for iPhone

Apple has no store for apps like Zen, so a signing app installs it. Everything happens on the phone.

Recommended

Install with Scarlet

Scarlet installs app files on your iPhone. You install it once, then open Zen’s file with it. When a new version is out, Zen tells you and shows the same steps.

  1. Get Scarlet

    In Safari open Scarlet’s own website and follow its install button. Use only that address: many look-alike sites exist, and none needs your Apple ID password.

    Open Scarlet’s website

  2. Trust it, if iOS asks

    Open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap the certificate Scarlet names and choose Trust.

  3. Download Zen

    Tap the button in Safari. The file Zen-unsigned.ipa is saved to the Files app.

    Download Zen for iPhone

  4. Install it with Scarlet

    In Scarlet press and hold the download arrow, choose Sideload IPA, pick Zen-unsigned.ipa and tap Install.

Honestly: Scarlet signs Zen with a certificate it provides. Apple can revoke such certificates; then Zen stops opening until you install it again the same way. We have not verified that this keeps your data, so make a full account copy first (Zen: Settings → Account and recovery). Zen’s own encryption does not depend on this certificate.

On iPhone Zen receives messages at once while it is open. Otherwise iOS lets it check for mail now and then, and until then messages wait safely with your friends. To receive all the time, turn it on in Settings → Background work (it uses more battery).

Why not the App Store? Its legal rules, such as moderation, payments and financial licences, need a company and money that Zen does not have. Why not in the stores

If something goes wrong

Scarlet will not open: “untrusted developer”

Do step 2 again: Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → Trust.

Zen stopped opening or Scarlet stopped working

The certificate was probably revoked. Update or reinstall Scarlet, then install Zen again from step 3. Do not delete Zen first if you can avoid it.

Scarlet does not work on my iOS version

Use one of the other ways below.

Other ways

Or add the Zen source to Scarlet: you then install new versions of Zen in Scarlet itself. If the button does nothing, in Scarlet press and hold the download arrow, choose Add Repo and paste https://robertostrowskijagoda.github.io/Zen/scarlet.json.
