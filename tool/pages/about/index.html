Zen

A messenger and social app for people who know each other.
No servers. No ads. No algorithm.

Download Zen

What it is

Zen connects you only with people you have met: you add a friend by scanning their QR code or with an invitation link. Every phone and computer is a node of a mesh network (Yggdrasil), so messages, calls and files go straight between your devices and your friends' — there is no company server that stores, reads or sells them.

What you can do

Chats and callsPrivate and group chats, voice and video calls, photos, videos, files, GIFs.
A feed of friendsPosts, photos and videos from your friends, in order — no algorithm decides what you see.
NearbySee which friends are close by over Bluetooth, knock on a neighbour's door, raise an alarm.
Meetings and eventsPlan meetings, share food, find people through friends of friends.
Works without internetBluetooth, Wi-Fi Direct and the local network carry messages when there is no connection.
Many devicesAndroid, iPhone, Windows, macOS and Linux; one account on several devices.

Privacy and security

  • End-to-end encryption with post-quantum algorithms (ML-KEM-768 and ML-DSA-65, combined with X25519 and Ed25519).
  • No phone number, no e-mail, no account on anyone's server.
  • Your data is encrypted on your device; an emergency wipe password erases everything.
  • Lost your phone? Friends you choose help you recover your account.
  • Every release is signed; the app checks the signature before it updates itself.

Get started

  • Install Zen on your phone or computer (the page shows the steps for your system).
  • Meet a friend who has Zen and scan each other's QR code, or open their invitation link.
  • Questions and ideas: Discord or GitHub issues.
