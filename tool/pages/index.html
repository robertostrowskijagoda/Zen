Why Zen is different

A messenger for people who actually know each other.

No servers

Messages go straight between your friends’ devices. No company stores, reads or sells them.

Friends as mailboxes

Up to six friends you trust keep your mail while you are offline — encrypted, so they can’t read it.

End-to-end encrypted

Post-quantum encryption protects every message, call and file. Only the people in the chat can read them.

Open source and free

No ads, no tracking, no algorithm. The whole code is on GitHub (GPL-3.0) for anyone to check.

See it in action

How to start

  1. Install Zen

    On your phone or computer. Free, no account.

  2. Add your friend

    Scan their QR code or open their invitation link — the app adds them for you.

  3. Say hello

    Chat, call and share. Everything stays between you and your friends.
