Why Zen

What you get, and what you don’t.

  1. Nobody reads your conversations

    No ads, no tracking, no algorithm. There is no company server in the middle: what you write goes to your friends encrypted end to end, and your data stays on your device.

  2. Only people you really know

    You add friends in person with a QR code, or with an invitation link. No phone number and no e-mail, so nobody can look you up or add you without your knowing.

  3. Free, and open for anyone to check

    No subscription, no paid version, no ads. The code is open (GPL-3.0), so anyone can see what Zen does, and every change is public.

  4. Ready for quantum computers

    Key exchanges and signatures combine today’s cryptography with the new NIST standards (ML-KEM-768, ML-DSA-65), so what someone records today stays unreadable tomorrow.

  5. Feed, groups and calls in one place

    The Feed shows your friends’ posts in order, only to the people they choose. Groups, voice and video calls with screen sharing, photos, files and voice notes.

  6. On all your devices

    Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Linux, one account on several devices. 27 looks, each light and dark, and 21 languages.

  7. A tool for deciding together

    The pyramid: small cells of people who know each other in person, where votes, trust and a common purse flow from the bottom up, with no headquarters to bribe or shut down.

Common questions

Is it safe?

Messages are encrypted end to end, with a post-quantum key exchange, and what Zen keeps on your device is encrypted with your password. The code is open, every release is signed and updates are checked before they are installed. The security model lists the limits honestly too.

Why isn’t it in Google Play or the App Store?

The stores require what Zen by design does not have: a company that moderates content on a server, payments through the store and control over updates. Zen is installed straight from GitHub and tells you itself when a new version is out. The reasons in detail

What if I lose my phone?

Set up a way back right away. You get your account back from another device with it, a full account copy (an encrypted file), a key copy (paper or file), or with your recovery circle of 4 to 6 friends. There is no “forgot password” e-mail, because no company holds your account.

Do my friends need Zen too?

Yes: Zen connects people who have it. Someone who already has Zen can send you the app straight from their phone, and then you add each other with a QR code.
