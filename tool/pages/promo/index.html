Polish

Zen to prywatna sieć społecznościowa dla ludzi, którzy się znają. Bez reklam, bez śledzenia, bez algorytmu i bez firmy pośrodku: wiadomości, rozmowy i posty idą prosto między urządzeniami znajomych, zaszyfrowane. Za darmo, z otwartym kodem, na Androida, iPhone’a, Windows, Maca i Linuksa. https://robertostrowskijagoda.github.io/Zen/about/

English

Zen is a private social network for people who know each other. No ads, no tracking, no algorithm and no company in the middle: messages, calls and posts go straight between friends’ devices, encrypted. Free and open source, for Android, iPhone, Windows, Mac and Linux. https://robertostrowskijagoda.github.io/Zen/about/

A QR code to this site

It leads to the about page. Put it on a poster, a flyer or a slide. The PDF is an A4 page with a square to cut out: the QR code, a short line asking to scan it and a small Zen signature.

Your own sticker from the app

In Zen, the About screen makes a promo sticker for you: a QR code that leads either to this site or straight to your own invitation, so whoever scans it can add you as a friend at once. Print it and put it where people you know will see it.
